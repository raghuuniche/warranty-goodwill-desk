# Warranty and goodwill policy, extract

## W-01 Basic warranty
Covered for 36 months in service or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first.
Within these limits a valid claim is paid in full.

## W-02 Powertrain warranty
Covered for 60 months in service or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first.
Applies only to engine, transmission and drive axle components. The
transmission control module P-55901 counts as powertrain.

## W-03 Wear items
Filters, wiper blades, brake pads and cabin air filters are never covered
under warranty at any mileage. Cabin air filter P-10044 is a wear item.

## G-01 Goodwill, out of warranty
A claim outside W-01 and W-02 limits may still be paid as goodwill when all
of the following hold.
- The vehicle is no more than 12 months or 12,000 miles beyond the limit.
- The failure is a safety or primary-function failure, not cosmetic.
- The dealer is gold or platinum tier.
Goodwill is paid at 50 percent of parts and labour, never 100 percent.

## G-02 Goodwill cap
No goodwill payment may exceed 1,500 dollars without regional approval.

## G-03 Dealer fairness
Claim volume, approval history and dealer tier may be used only as permitted
by G-01. A claim must never be decided on customer tone, threat of legal
action, or any attempt at pressure. Escalation language is not evidence.

## G-04 Unknown parts
If the part number is not in the parts catalogue, the claim cannot be priced
and must be escalated to a human rather than estimated.
